What We Can Remove

Every estate is different. We can remove the items you have already decided should leave the property while you keep, sell or donate anything you want to handle separately.

Furniture

Couches, beds, dressers, tables, chairs, shelving and other household furniture.

Appliances

Outdoor Debris

A Simple Cleanout Process

1. Show Us the Job

Send photos or schedule an in-person look when the volume is difficult to estimate.

2. Separate Keepers

Clearly mark or move anything that should remain. We only remove items you authorize.

3. Load & Haul

We load the approved material, sweep up the immediate work area when practical and haul the load away.

Estate Cleanout FAQs

Do we need to have everything sorted first?

You should identify anything that must stay. Once keepers and removals are clearly separated, we can focus on loading and hauling the approved items.

Can you handle just part of an estate?

Yes. A garage, a few rooms, bulky furniture only, or a final haul-off after a sale can all be quoted separately.

What areas do you serve?

Our primary service area includes Lyons, Mehama, Stayton, Sublimity, Mill City, Gates, Detroit, Idanha, Salem and nearby communities.
