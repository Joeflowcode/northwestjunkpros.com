Estate Sale & Cleanout FAQs

Do we need to sort the entire house before contacting you?

No. You should identify important personal items, documents and anything that must stay, but you can contact us before the rest of the property is completely sorted.

Can you help if we only need an estate cleanout?

Yes. If selling is already handled or the remaining contents simply need removed, we can quote a partial or complete estate cleanout.

Can you help after another estate sale company finishes?

Yes. Final haul-off and cleanout work can be quoted separately after a sale.

What areas do you serve?

Our primary service area includes Salem, Stayton, Sublimity, Lyons, Mehama, Mill City, Gates, Detroit, Idanha and surrounding Santiam Canyon communities.

How do we get started?

Send several wide photos of the rooms, garage, shop or property along with the city and a short explanation of the situation. We will use that information to discuss the next step.
