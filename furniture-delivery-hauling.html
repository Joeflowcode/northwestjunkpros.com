Furniture Delivery FAQs

Can you pick up something I buy on Facebook Marketplace?

Yes, if the item, route and schedule work. Send the listing photo, pickup address, delivery address and dimensions before arranging the job.

Can you move items upstairs?

Possibly. Stairs and tight access need to be disclosed in advance so we can confirm the job is practical and quote the extra handling time.

Do you offer full household moving?

Our focus is hauling, delivery and large-item transport rather than full-service interstate or whole-house moving.
